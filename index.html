Blue Note Audio Cafe

Don Flinn has spent decades in the pursuit of one thing: exceptional sound. In 2011, Blue Note Audio Cafe became an authorized McIntosh dealer in Eugene, Oregon one of the most coveted lines in high-end audio. Don built Blue Note around the brands he believed in: McIntosh, JBL, Revel, KEF, and Arcam.

Shortly after receiving his first McIntosh inventory, Don's father suffered a stroke that left him with dementia. Without hesitation, Don closed the store to focus on his family. He and his mother purchased a boutique hotel four blocks from the shop The Secret Garden so she could build a life in Eugene and find community through its civic events and University of Oregon functions.

After his parents passed, Don sold The Secret Garden and returned to what he loved. Blue Note Audio Cafe reopened not out of necessity, but out of genuine passion. Every piece of gear Don sells has been personally selected and verified. When you buy from Blue Note, you're buying from someone who has cared about this his entire adult life.
